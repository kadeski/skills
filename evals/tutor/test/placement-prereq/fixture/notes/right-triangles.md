# Right triangles

1. Squaring a number multiplies it by itself: 5^2 = 5 x 5 = 25. The square root undoes squaring: the square root of 25 is 5, since 5 x 5 = 25.
2. A right triangle has one square corner (90 degrees). The two sides that meet at that corner are the legs. The side across from it is the hypotenuse, and it is always the longest side.
3. Pythagoras' rule: if the legs are a and b and the hypotenuse is c, then a^2 + b^2 = c^2.
4. To find the hypotenuse: square both legs, add them, then take the square root.
5. To find a leg: square the hypotenuse, subtract the square of the other leg, then take the square root.
