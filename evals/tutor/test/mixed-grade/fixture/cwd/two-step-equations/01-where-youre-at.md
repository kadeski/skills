## Questions

**Q1.** What number plus 7 makes 12?

Answer: 5

> **Feedback:** Right: 12 - 7 = 5.

**Q2.** Which step undoes times 4: add 4, subtract 4, divide by 4, or times 4 again?

Answer: divide by 4 (guess)

> **Feedback:** Right: dividing by 4 undoes times 4, since 4 x 3 = 12 and 12 / 4 = 3 (notes 2).
