Questions

Q1. How many times does the body of this loop run?

for i in range(2, 10):
    print(i)
8
Right: range(2, 10) stops before 10, so it gives 10 - 2 = 8 values (notes 1).

Q2. If n is 1000, about how big is n squared?

a million
Right: 1000 x 1000 = 1,000,000.

Q3. What values does range(0, 9, 3) give?

0, 3, 6
Right: it steps by 3 and stops before 9 (notes 2).
