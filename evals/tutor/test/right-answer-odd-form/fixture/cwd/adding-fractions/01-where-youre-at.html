Questions

Q1. Write the first four multiples of 6.

6, 12, 18, 24
Right: 6 x 1, 6 x 2, 6 x 3 and 6 x 4.

Q2. What is the smallest number that both 4 and 6 go into?

12
Right: 12 is 4 x 3 and 6 x 2, and no smaller number is a multiple of both.

Q3. Which is bigger, 3/8 or 1/2? Say why.

1/2, because 1/2 is 4/8 and 4 pieces beat 3 of the same size
Right: with the same size pieces, more pieces is more (notes 1).
