## Rules

- r02 angles on a straight line add up to 180 degrees - learned
- r03 angles around a point add up to 360 degrees - next {{d-2}}, gap 4
- r04 where two straight lines cross, opposite angles are equal - next {{d-1}}, gap 8
- r05 the three angles in a triangle add up to 180 degrees - next {{d-1}}, gap 2
- r06 the two base angles of an isosceles triangle are equal - next {{d}}, gap 2

## Misconceptions

- 03 guess: thinks the angle opposite makes 180 with it, like the angle beside it.
- 05 guess: thinks the angle between the equal sides equals the base angles.
- 06 guess: gives the inside angle at the corner, not the outside one.
