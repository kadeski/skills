# Angle facts

1. Angles are measured in degrees. A full turn is 360 degrees, a half turn is 180 and a quarter turn (a right angle) is 90.
2. Angles that sit side by side on a straight line add up to 180 degrees, a half turn.
3. Angles around a point add up to 360 degrees, a full turn.
4. When two straight lines cross, the angles opposite each other are equal. Angles next to each other add to 180, since they sit on a straight line.
5. The three angles inside a triangle add up to 180 degrees.
6. An isosceles triangle has two equal sides. The two angles where those sides meet the third side, the base angles, are equal.
7. Extend one side of a triangle past a corner. The outside angle it makes equals the two inside angles at the other corners added together.
