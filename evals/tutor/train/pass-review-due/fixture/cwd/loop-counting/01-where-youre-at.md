## Questions

**Q1.** How many times does the body of this loop run?

```python
for i in range(2, 10):
    print(i)
```

Answer: 8

> **Feedback:** Right: `range(2, 10)` stops before 10, so it gives 10 - 2 = 8 values (notes 1).

**Q2.** If n is 1000, about how big is n squared?

Answer: a million

> **Feedback:** Right: 1000 x 1000 = 1,000,000.

**Q3.** What values does `range(0, 9, 3)` give?

Answer: 0, 3, 6

> **Feedback:** Right: it steps by 3 and stops before 9 (notes 2).
