## Questions

**Q1.** How many centimetres are in 1 metre?

Answer: 100

> **Feedback:** Right: centi means one hundredth, so 100 cm make a metre (notes 1).

**Q2.** What is 4.5 x 100?

Answer: 450

> **Feedback:** Right: x 100 moves each digit two places to the left.

**Q3.** What does the "kilo" in kilogram mean?

Answer:

> **Feedback:** Kilo means 1000 of the unit after it. A kilowatt is 1000 watts, and a kilobyte was named the same way (notes 1). You will use it again in lesson 04.
