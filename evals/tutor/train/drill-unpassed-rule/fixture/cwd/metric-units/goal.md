## Rules

- r02 to change to a smaller unit, multiply by how many small units fit in one big one - next {{d-3}}, gap 2
- r03 to change to a bigger unit, divide by how many small units fit in one big one - next {{d-2}}, gap 2
- r04 masses take the same prefixes: 1 kg = 1000 g and 1 g = 1000 mg - next {{d}}, gap 2

## Misconceptions

- 03 guess: thinks milli means one hundredth, like centi.
- 04 guess: changes km to m and stops, as if that reaches cm.
