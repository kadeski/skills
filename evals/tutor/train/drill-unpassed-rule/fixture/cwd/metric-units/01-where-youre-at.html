Questions

Q1. How many centimetres are in 1 metre?

100
Right: centi means one hundredth, so 100 cm make a metre (notes 1).

Q2. What is 4.5 x 100?

450
Right: x 100 moves each digit two places to the left.

Q3. What does the "kilo" in kilogram mean?

Kilo means 1000 of the unit after it. A kilowatt is 1000 watts, and a kilobyte was named the same way (notes 1). You will use it again in lesson 04.
