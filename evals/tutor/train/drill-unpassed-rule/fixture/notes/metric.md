# Metric units

1. Length units are built from the metre (m) with a prefix: kilo means 1000, centi means one hundredth, milli means one thousandth. So 1 km = 1000 m, 1 m = 100 cm, 1 m = 1000 mm, and 1 cm = 10 mm.
2. Mass works the same way with the gram (g): 1 kg = 1000 g, and 1 g = 1000 mg.
3. To change to a smaller unit, multiply by how many small units fit in one big one: 3 m = 3 x 100 = 300 cm. The number gets bigger because the unit got smaller.
4. To change to a bigger unit, divide by how many small units fit in one big one: 450 cm = 450 / 100 = 4.5 m.
5. To cross more than one step, change through the middle unit, or multiply the factors first: km to cm is x 1000 and then x 100, so x 100,000 in one go.
6. Area units are squares, so the factor is squared: 1 m = 100 cm, so 1 m² = 100 x 100 = 10,000 cm².
7. Volume units are cubes, so the factor is cubed: 1 m³ = 100 x 100 x 100 = 1,000,000 cm³. A litre is 1000 cm³.
