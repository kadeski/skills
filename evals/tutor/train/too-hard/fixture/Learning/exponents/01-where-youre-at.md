Answer: 16

**Q2.** Write 3 x 3 x 3 x 3 x 3 with an exponent.

Answer: 3^5 (guess)

> **Feedback:** Right: five 3s multiplied together is 3 to the power 5 (notes 1).

**Q3.** What is half of a half?

Answer: a quarter

> **Feedback:** Right: 1/2 x 1/2 = 1/4.
