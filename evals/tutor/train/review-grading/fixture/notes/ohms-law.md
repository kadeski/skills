# Ohm's law and series circuits

1. Current, I, is the flow of charge, measured in amperes (A). Voltage, V, is the push that drives it, measured in volts (V). Resistance, R, opposes the flow, measured in ohms.
2. Ohm's law: V = I x R. Rearranged, I = V / R and R = V / I.
3. A series circuit has one path, so the same current flows through every part of it.
4. Resistances in series add: R_total = R1 + R2 + R3 and so on.
5. The battery's voltage is shared among the parts in series. Each part's voltage drop is I x R for that part, and the drops add up to the battery voltage.
6. Power is P = V x I, measured in watts (W).
