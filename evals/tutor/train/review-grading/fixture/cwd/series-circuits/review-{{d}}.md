## Review

**R1.** A 6 V battery drives 2 A through a lamp. What is the lamp's resistance, in ohms?

Answer: 3 (sure)

**R2.** A 4 ohm and an 8 ohm resistor sit in series on a 12 V battery. Which one carries more current?

- a) the 4 ohm one
- b) the 8 ohm one
- c) both carry the same current
- d) whichever comes first after the battery

Answer: a, less resistance lets more current through (sure)

**R3.** Resistors of 5, 10 and 15 ohms sit in series. What is the total resistance, in ohms?

Answer: 30
