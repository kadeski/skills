## Rules

- r02 V = I x R, so I = V / R and R = V / I - next {{d}}, gap 4
- r03 a series circuit has one path, so the same current flows through every part - next {{d}}, gap 2
- r04 resistances in series add - next {{d}}, gap 16
- r05 each part drops I x R, and the drops add up to the battery voltage - next {{d+3}}, gap 4
