# Mean and median

1. The mean adds up all the values and divides by how many there are.
2. The median is the middle value once the values are sorted. With an even count, it is halfway between the two middle values.
3. An outlier is a value far from the rest. It pulls the mean toward it, since its whole size goes into the total, but it barely moves the median, which only depends on order.
4. So a high outlier puts the mean above the median, and a low outlier puts it below.
5. When a list has outliers, the median says more about a typical value than the mean does.
