## Questions

**Q1.** How many times does the body of this loop run?

```python
for i in range(2, 10):
    print(i)
```

Answer: 9 times: 2, 3, 4 and so on up to 10 (sure)

**Q2.** If n is 1000, about how big is n squared?

Answer: 2000 (guess)

**Q3.** What values does `range(0, 9, 3)` give?

Answer: 0, 3, 6
