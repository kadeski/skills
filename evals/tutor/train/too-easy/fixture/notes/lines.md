# Straight lines

1. Slope is rise over run: how far a line goes up for each one step to the right. A line that goes down to the right has a negative rise, so a negative slope.
2. Through two points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2), the slope is m = (y2 - y1) / (x2 - x1). Subtract in the same order on top and bottom; which point you call first does not matter.
3. A line can be written y = mx + b, where m is the slope and b is where the line crosses the y-axis, the value of y when x is 0.
4. To find b, put the slope and one point on the line into y = mx + b, then solve for b.
5. Two lines with the same slope are parallel.
