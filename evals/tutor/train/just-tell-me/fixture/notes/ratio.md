# Ratios

1. A ratio a:b compares two amounts: for every a of the first there are b of the second.
2. Multiplying or dividing both sides by the same number gives an equal ratio: 6:4 = 3:2.
3. To share an amount in the ratio a:b, count a + b equal shares, find one share by dividing the amount by a + b, then multiply it by a and by b.
4. The shares always add back to the whole amount, which makes a quick check.
5. To scale a recipe, multiply every amount by the same factor: the new number of people divided by the old.
