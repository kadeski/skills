Questions

Q1. What is 36 divided by 9?

4
Right: 9 x 4 = 36.

Q2. A bag holds 3 red beads and 5 blue ones. What fraction of the beads are red?

3/8
Right: 3 red out of 3 + 5 = 8 beads.

Q3. What is 3/5 of 40?

24
Right: 40 / 5 = 8, and 3 x 8 = 24.
