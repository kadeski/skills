## Questions

**Q1.** What is 36 divided by 9?

Answer: 4

> **Feedback:** Right: 9 x 4 = 36.

**Q2.** A bag holds 3 red beads and 5 blue ones. What fraction of the beads are red?

Answer: 3/8

> **Feedback:** Right: 3 red out of 3 + 5 = 8 beads.

**Q3.** What is 3/5 of 40?

Answer: 24

> **Feedback:** Right: 40 / 5 = 8, and 3 x 8 = 24.
