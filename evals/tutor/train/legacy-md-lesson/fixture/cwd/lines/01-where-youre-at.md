Part of [Straight Lines From Two Points](goal.md). Add `sure` or `guess` after any answer, and say `done` when finished.

## Questions

**Q1.** What is 7 - (-3)?

Answer: 10

> **Feedback:** Right: taking away -3 is the same as adding 3.

**Q2.** What is -8 / 4?

Answer: -2 (guess)

> **Feedback:** Right: 8 / 4 = 2, and a minus divided by a plus is a minus.

**Q3.** On a grid you walk from (1, 1) to (4, 1). How far did you move, and which way?

Answer: 3 to the right

> **Feedback:** Right: x went from 1 to 4 and y stayed at 1.
