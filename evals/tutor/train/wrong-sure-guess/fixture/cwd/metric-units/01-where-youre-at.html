Q1. How many cm are in 1 m?

100
Right: 1 m = 100 cm (notes 1).

Q2. What is 2.5 x 100?

250
Right: times 100 moves each digit two places left.

Q3. A rectangle is 3 cm by 5 cm. What is its area?

15 cm² (sure)
Right: 3 x 5 = 15 squares of 1 cm by 1 cm.
