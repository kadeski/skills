# Metric units

1. Length: 1 km = 1000 m, 1 m = 100 cm, 1 cm = 10 mm.
2. To change to a smaller unit, multiply by the factor between them. To change to a bigger unit, divide. So 3.5 m = 350 cm, and 70 mm = 7 cm.
3. A converted length is the same length in a different unit: a smaller unit needs more of them.
4. Area units are squares. 1 m² is a square 1 m by 1 m, which is 100 cm by 100 cm, so 1 m² = 100 x 100 = 10,000 cm². The area factor is the length factor squared.
5. In the same way, 1 cm² = 10 x 10 = 100 mm², and 1 km² = 1000 x 1000 = 1,000,000 m².
6. Volume units are cubes. 1 m³ = 100 x 100 x 100 = 1,000,000 cm³: the volume factor is the length factor cubed. 1 litre is 1000 cm³.
