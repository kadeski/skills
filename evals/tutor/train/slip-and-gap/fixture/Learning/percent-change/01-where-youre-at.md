## Questions

**Q1.** Write 3/4 as a decimal.

Answer: 0.75

> **Feedback:** Right: 3 divided by 4 is 0.75.

**Q2.** What is 0.2 x 45?

Answer: 9 (guess)

> **Feedback:** Right. 0.2 is 2 tenths, and a tenth of 45 is 4.5, so two tenths is 9.

**Q3.** Which is bigger, 0.6 or 0.58? Say why.

Answer: 0.6, because 0.60 is more than 0.58 once both have two decimal places

> **Feedback:** Right: writing both with two places makes them easy to compare.
